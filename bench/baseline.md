# Baseline — fitted per-invoke cost model (M1.5 snapshot)

Generated by `bench/run.mjs` (tiers 1–2) per `docs/evaluation.md`.
Every number below is reproducible with `node bench/run.mjs`. This snapshot
**supersedes the v1 baseline** (which contained an Amdahl arithmetic error —
see §Analysis — and reported `fanout` in the wrong unit).

| date | 2026-09-19 |

The "no journal" side of every pair is the explicitly **non-conforming**
`bench: true` sink (constant-time hashing; events are still generated), not a
removed journal — F7 of the external review deleted the `journal:false`
seam. All paired variants run the identical mediated workload.

## Tier 1 — cost model (best-of-two-pass medians, ns/op)

| variant | median | min | max | what it isolates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `raw` | **35.2** | 25.8 | 2 031 | unmediated async call (honest zero line) |
| `cap_bench` | **2 489** | 1 572 | 4 587 | full mediation minus crypto: WeakMap token lookup, rights checks, structured clone, promise plumbing, journal event construction |
| `cap_journal` | **9 018** | 8 640 | 12 042 | + canonicalization + SHA-256 (args/result/chain) + chained append |
| `journal_m1` | 9 242 | 7 345 | 11 889 | one membrane (Δ vs `cap_journal` = noise-level) |
| `journal_m3` | 8 650 | 5 940 | 11 234 | three membranes (same — marginal membrane cost is unmeasurable) |
| `ipc` (mediated tell+recv) | 6 861 | 5 225 | 9 961 | one queue pair incl. clone + sender-slot mediation scan + 2 journal appends |
| `derive` (spawn+grant) | 6 049 | 4 470 | 11 408 | one delegation step incl. cap mint + journal append |

### Fitted coefficients

```
c_cap     ≈ 2.45 µs   mediation logic itself (cap_journal − cap_bench ≠ this;
                      see split below: 2.49 µs is the non-crypto floor)
c_journal ≈ 6.53 µs   audit crypto + append (cap_journal − cap_bench)
c_mem     ≈ 0         (per membrane; ≤ 0.3 µs, inside run-to-run noise)
c_ipc     ≈ 6.9 µs    mediated tell+recv roundtrip, journaling on
c_derive  ≈ 6.0 µs    grant step, journaling on
memory    ≈ 1 018 B   journal retained per mediated invoke (deep-size
                      estimate of entries[], not process RSS)
```

**The journal is still the cost, but less absolutely than v1 claimed**:
the audit share of a mediated invoke is
f = (9 018 − 2 489) / 9 018 ≈ **72%** (v1 said 87%; v1's `cap_nojournal`
under-measured the mediation path because it lacked structured cloning and
the canonical serializer). The mediation floor itself grew from ~1 µs (v1)
to ~2.5 µs in v1.5 — that is the in-realm price of WeakMap tokens,
clone-on-boundary, and total hashing (findings F1–F13).

## Amdahl before optimizing (charter §4)

Paired measurement on the full-kernel-shaped workload (`tool_loop_*`):
audit share f = (8 923 − 2 932) / 8 923 ≈ **0.67**.

- 2× faster digest: `1 / ((1−0.67) + 0.67/2) ≈ 1.51×` end-to-end.
- Complete elimination of audit crypto: upper bound `1/(1−0.67) ≈ 3.0×`.
- Halving the mediation floor (2.5 µs of the 8.9 µs): ≤ 1.16× — not worth
  security loss.

The v1 document claimed "1.85× from 2× hashing at f≈0.88, while also
calling the capability check 0.06%" — the f and the fraction were computed
from different runs and the ratio arithmetic was wrong. Numbers above are
from one session, paired and interleaved.

Priority stands: attack the hash path first —
1. **batch the chain hash** (one digest over k events; per-batch tamper
   evidence, weakens per-event chaining — needs an invariant #10 wording
   check before adoption);
2. stream `argsHash`/`resultHash` only when a consumer is attached;
3. leave membranes alone — measured free at n ≤ 3.

## M5 implications (enforcement-backend decision)

- v1.5 overhead is still crypto-dominated (~72% of a mediated call), so a
  kernel backend cannot win on this axis unless it changes *where* and *how
  often* hashing happens. Judge backends on isolation properties (#5, #7,
  #8) and re-measure at tier 3 (charter §2: microkernel numbers may not
  justify a backend decision — stated explicitly per the "benchmarks always
  valid" fallacy).
- The queue-only floor (~1.2 µs/op with clone + mediation, no crypto) says
  what semantic work costs before any transport: M3 worker/child-process
  transports will add serialization + syscall cost on top of this, and that
  delta is the honest transport-budget question. Do **not** extrapolate
  these µs figures to a kernel ABI.

## Caveats (charter §3 honesty clause)

- Measured in an embedded Node REPL (single process, warm JIT, noisy shared
  laptop). No standalone `node.exe` was available in this environment, so
  "Node v24.18.0 (embedded-REPL harness)" is the runtime of record; a
  `node bench/run.mjs` run on a clean install is the reproduction path and
  may shift medians by ~±10%. Interleaved pass deltas of up to ~17%
  (e.g. `cap_journal` 10 536 → 9 018) are visible in the raw pass numbers;
  best-of-two-pass medians are reported, and *max* is the tail story under
  co-tenant load.
- Cooperative timing: microtask granularity inflates the `raw` floor;
  differences between paired variants, not absolutes, carry conclusions.
- Memory is a deep-size estimate over journal entries (64 B/object header
  assumption); it excludes retained cap/actor graphs and V8 fragmentation.

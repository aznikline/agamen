### Fitted coefficients (medians, two independent rounds ±10%)

```
c_cap   ≈   0.95 µs   (capability check + admission plumbing, no journal)
c_journal ≈ 6.6 µs    (per mediated call: argsHash + resultHash + chain hash)
c_mem   ≲   0.3 µs    (per membrane; point estimate is inside run noise:
                       m3 ≈ m1 ≈ cap_journal, so the marginal check is nearly free)
c_ipc   ≈   4.3 µs    (tell+recv pair, journaling on; pure queue glue is
                       indistinguishable from its ~2 journal appends)
c_derive ≈ per-op values in table; dominated by journal appends as well
```

**The journal is the cost**: `c_journal / mediated ≈ 87%` of a mediated
invoke; the capability machinery itself is under 1 µs. Crypto-hashing —
not rights checks — is the price of provenance in v1.

## Amdahl before optimizing (charter §4)

Journal ≈ 88% of `tool_loop`. A 2× faster digest buys
`1/((1−0.88)+0.88/2) ≈ 1.85×` end-to-end — worth doing. Halving the
capability check (0.06%→…) buys nothing. First candidates, in order of
measured leverage:

1. **batch the chain hash** (one digest over k events instead of k) — attacks
   the dominant term without dropping the tamper-evidence property per batch;
2. stream `argsHash`/`resultHash` only when a consumer is attached;
3. leave membranes alone — they are already ~free.

## M5 implications (enforcement-backend decision)

- v1 overhead is crypto, not mediation logic; a kernel backend can't win on
  this axis unless it changes *where* the hashing happens. Judge backends
  on isolation properties (#5, #7, #8) and measure again at tier 3.
- `c_ipc` floor says pure actor messaging is cheap (µs → 100 ns class);
  anything slower than ~1 µs per hop in a future transport is implementation
  waste, not semantic cost. This is the budget M3 transports must fit inside.

## Caveats (charter §3 honesty clause)

- Measured in an embedded Node REPL (single process, warm JIT, noisy shared
  laptop): treat medians as ±10% and *max* as the tail-latency story under
  co-tenant load. `fanout` uses the `journal:false` measurement seam.
- Cooperative timing: JS microtask granularity inflates the `raw` floor;
  differences, not absolutes, carry the conclusions.
- Variance across reps exceeded 15% for `journal_m1/m3/ipc/derive`;
  medians are reported as the summary per charter, max shown for tails.
